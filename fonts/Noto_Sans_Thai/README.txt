Noto Sans Thai
==============

NotoSansThai-VariableFont_wdth,wght.ttf is Google Fonts' variable Noto Sans
Thai (google/fonts, ofl/notosansthai, commit 47d54ca1ebb1), licensed under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). Its default instance is the regular
weight at normal width.

Shells register it beside Noto Sans JP as a fallback face, so Thai text —
BAHTTEXT's result among it — draws in Thai glyphs rather than the Japanese
face's missing-glyph boxes.
